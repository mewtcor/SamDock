Nature Home for Samsung Tizen 6.0

Open index.html on the PC to preview the layout. Click a dock tile to see the preview message. App launching works only after the signed app is installed on the TV.

The first dock tile is Stremio. On the TV, Nature Home looks up installed apps by name and launches the matching app. If Stremio is not installed, its tile says "Not installed" and directs you to Samsung Apps.

The three included nature images rotate once per minute. No images or usage data are sent to a server.

Nature Home is a separate app. It does not replace Samsung Home or change what the remote's Home button does. It does not remove or modify existing apps.

Installation requires Tizen Studio with the Samsung TV and Certificate extensions, a Samsung Developers account, and a Samsung certificate tied to this TV's DUID. The source folder is not a signed, installable WGT package.
